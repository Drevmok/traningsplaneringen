## Gathering (6)

### gather-name-circle
- **title:** Name circle  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Everyone says their name and one energy word so the group feels seen.  
- **howTo:** Stand or sit in a circle. Go one by one: name + one word (excited, tired, ready). Coach models first.  
- **watchFor:** Skip anyone who freezes — offer “pass” and come back later. Keep it light.  
- **visualKey:** gather-name-circle  
- **stub:** true

### gather-today-goal
- **title:** Today’s one goal  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Share one focus for the session so athletes know what “good” looks like today.  
- **howTo:** Coach names the session goal in one sentence. Ask 2–3 athletes to repeat it in their own words.  
- **watchFor:** Keep goals doable for this group. Avoid long speeches.  
- **visualKey:** gather-today-goal  
- **stub:** true

### gather-attendance-high-five
- **title:** Attendance high-fives  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Quick check-in that builds energy and confirms who is here.  
- **howTo:** Call names or have athletes high-five a partner as they arrive in the circle. Count the group out loud.  
- **watchFor:** Include late arrivals without stopping the whole plan.  
- **visualKey:** gather-high-five  
- **stub:** true

### gather-listen-up
- **title:** Listen-up signal  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Teach a quiet signal you will reuse all session.  
- **howTo:** Demonstrate the signal (hand up, clap pattern, or freeze). Practice twice. Praise the first quiet moment.  
- **watchFor:** Don’t shout over noise — wait, then use the signal.  
- **visualKey:** gather-listen  
- **stub:** true

### gather-partner-find
- **title:** Find a partner  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Pair athletes for the next block without chaos.  
- **howTo:** Call “find a partner.” Help leftovers join a pair of three. Confirm pairs with a wave.  
- **watchFor:** Watch for anyone left out; coach assigns quickly and kindly.  
- **visualKey:** gather-partners  
- **stub:** true

### gather-safety-spot
- **title:** Safety spot reminder  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Point out boundaries, landing areas, and where to wait.  
- **howTo:** Walk or point: mats, runways, wait line, water. Ask one athlete: “Where do we wait?”  
- **watchFor:** Keep it under two minutes. Repeat only what matters for today’s setup.  
- **visualKey:** gather-safety  
- **stub:** true

## Warm-up (6)

### warm-jog-lines
- **title:** Easy jog lines  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`, `floor`  
- **summary:** Light jogging to raise heart rate without rushing skills.  
- **howTo:** Jog the floor length or a marked path. Soft landings. Option: high knees on the last length.  
- **watchFor:** No racing. Stop anyone sprinting into others.  
- **visualKey:** warm-jog  
- **stub:** true

### warm-animal-walks
- **title:** Animal walks  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 4  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`, `floor`  
- **summary:** Playful movement that wakes shoulders, hips, and core.  
- **howTo:** Bear walk, crab walk, frog hops — short distances. Demo each once.  
- **watchFor:** Wrists sore → shorten or switch to standing shapes. Keep space between athletes.  
- **visualKey:** warm-animals  
- **stub:** true

### warm-arm-circles
- **title:** Arm circles and reaches  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Gentle shoulder warm-up before supporting or swinging.  
- **howTo:** Small circles → bigger circles both ways. Reach up and side. Breathe out on the reach.  
- **watchFor:** Pain (not stretch) → stop that athlete’s circles and modify.  
- **visualKey:** warm-arms  
- **stub:** true

### warm-leg-swings
- **title:** Supported leg swings  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Loosen hips with controlled swings.  
- **howTo:** Hold a wall or partner’s shoulder. Swing forward/back, then side lightly. Switch legs.  
- **watchFor:** No kicking hard. Keep torso quiet.  
- **visualKey:** warm-legs  
- **stub:** true

### warm-jumping-jacks
- **title:** Jumping jacks  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Quick full-body pulse raiser.  
- **howTo:** Start slow, then find a steady rhythm. Offer step-jacks for anyone who needs lower impact.  
- **watchFor:** Knees soft. Space so elbows don’t collide.  
- **visualKey:** warm-jacks  
- **stub:** true

### warm-mobility-flow
- **title:** Mini mobility flow  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 4  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`, `floor`  
- **summary:** A short sequence: squat sit, cat-cow, and a gentle side reach (coach demo).  
- **howTo:** Coach leads 3–4 positions, 3 breaths each. Athletes copy at their range.  
- **watchFor:** No forcing depth. Modify to seated if needed.  
- **visualKey:** warm-mobility  
- **stub:** true

## Techniques (6) — stubs pending coach review

### tech-hollow-hold
- **title:** Hollow body hold  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `floor`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Shape work that supports many floor and bar skills.  
- **howTo:** On back, ribs down, legs and arms long. Hold short sets with rest.  
- **watchFor:** Lower back arching off the floor — bend knees or shorten hold. Breathe.  
- **visualKey:** tech-hollow  
- **stub:** true  
- **needsCoachReview:** true

### tech-tight-body-rock
- **title:** Tight body rocks  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `floor`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Teach squeeze and control before bigger skills.  
- **howTo:** Lie on back or front, body long and tight, small rocks. Cue “toes and fingers long.”  
- **watchFor:** Floppy necks or bent knees — reset and shrink the rock.  
- **visualKey:** tech-rocks  
- **stub:** true  
- **needsCoachReview:** true

### tech-handstand-prep-lunge
- **title:** Handstand entry lunge (prep)  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 5  
- **difficulty:** medium  
- **tags:** `floor`  
- **summary:** Safe entry practice toward handstand without requiring a full hold.  
- **howTo:** Lunge, hands to floor, kick to a controlled light touch or cartwheel-out as coached. Spot as needed.  
- **watchFor:** Do not force a hold. Clear landing space. Stop if wrists hurt.  
- **visualKey:** tech-hs-prep  
- **stub:** true  
- **needsCoachReview:** true

### tech-cartwheel-progress
- **title:** Cartwheel progressions  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 6  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `floor`  
- **summary:** Sideways travel with hands down, broken into steps.  
- **howTo:** Star shape → hand placement drill → slow cartwheel with spot if needed.  
- **watchFor:** Head looking the wrong way; rushing. Use soft mats.  
- **visualKey:** tech-cartwheel  
- **stub:** true  
- **needsCoachReview:** true

### tech-beam-walks
- **title:** Low beam walks  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 5  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `beam`  
- **summary:** Balance and posture on a low surface.  
- **howTo:** Forward walks, passé holds, turnaround. Arms helpful for balance.  
- **watchFor:** Only use height appropriate for the group. Spotters ready on first tries.  
- **visualKey:** tech-beam-walk  
- **stub:** true  
- **needsCoachReview:** true

### tech-vault-run-punch
- **title:** Vault run and punch prep  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 5  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `vault`  
- **summary:** Straight run and quick punch without full vaulting yet.  
- **howTo:** Run to board or marked zone, punch and stick or rebound as coached.  
- **watchFor:** One athlete at a time on the runway. Clear the landing.  
- **visualKey:** tech-vault-prep  
- **stub:** true  
- **needsCoachReview:** true

## Strength (6)

### str-Superman
- **title:** Superman lifts  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `floor`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Back and shoulder strength on the floor.  
- **howTo:** On stomach, lift arms and legs lightly. Short holds or pulses. Rest between sets.  
- **watchFor:** No cranking the neck up. Keep lifts small.  
- **visualKey:** str-superman  
- **stub:** true

### str-push-up-knee
- **title:** Knee push-ups  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `floor`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Upper-body strength with an accessible progression.  
- **howTo:** Hands under shoulders, body in a line from knees. Lower and press. Offer wall push-ups as regression.  
- **watchFor:** Hips sagging — shorten range or change to wall.  
- **visualKey:** str-pushup  
- **stub:** true

### str-boat-hold
- **title:** Boat / V sit hold  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `floor`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Core hold that transfers to many shapes.  
- **howTo:** Balance on sit bones, legs and arms up as able. Short holds.  
- **watchFor:** Rounded strain in the back — bend knees or feet down.  
- **visualKey:** str-boat  
- **stub:** true

### str-squat-hold
- **title:** Squat hold  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 2  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Leg strength and posture in a simple hold.  
- **howTo:** Feet planted, sit back as if to a chair. Hold, then stand tall.  
- **watchFor:** Knees collapsing in — cue “knees over toes track.” Heels down if possible.  
- **visualKey:** str-squat  
- **stub:** true

### str-hollow-rocks-strength
- **title:** Hollow rocks (strength)  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** medium  
- **tags:** `floor`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Stronger hollow variation for short sets.  
- **howTo:** Hollow shape, rock gently. Count rocks or time. Rest fully between sets.  
- **watchFor:** Same as hollow hold — protect the lower back; regress early.  
- **visualKey:** str-hollow-rocks  
- **stub:** true  
- **needsCoachReview:** true

### str-partner-resist
- **title:** Partner resistance stands  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 4  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Light partner pushes for posture and strength without equipment.  
- **howTo:** Pair up. One athlete stands strong while partner gives gentle pressure on shoulders or hands as coached. Switch.  
- **watchFor:** No sudden shoves. Match sizes when you can. Stop at form loss.  
- **visualKey:** str-partner  
- **stub:** true

## Fun and games (6)

### fun-freeze-dance
- **title:** Freeze dance  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 4  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Music or clap rhythm — dance, then freeze on signal.  
- **howTo:** Move freely on the floor. On signal, freeze in a strong shape. Resume.  
- **watchFor:** Clear boundaries so nobody crashes. Offer quiet freezes for overstimulated athletes.  
- **visualKey:** fun-freeze  
- **stub:** true

### fun-relay-bear
- **title:** Bear-walk relay  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 5  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `floor`  
- **summary:** Team relay with animal walks to keep energy high.  
- **howTo:** Two or more lines. Bear walk to a cone and back, tag next. Celebrate every team.  
- **watchFor:** No diving finishes. Keep lanes clear.  
- **visualKey:** fun-relay  
- **stub:** true

### fun-simon-says-shapes
- **title:** Simon says — gym shapes  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 4  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Listening game using stretch and tight shapes.  
- **howTo:** Call shapes with and without “Simon says.” Keep rounds short.  
- **watchFor:** Kindness if someone is out — reset often so nobody sits long.  
- **visualKey:** fun-simon  
- **stub:** true

### fun-balloon-keep-up
- **title:** Balloon keep-up  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 4  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`  
- **equipment:** `balloon`  
- **summary:** Cooperative keep-the-balloon-up game.  
- **howTo:** One or more balloons. Group taps gently to keep them off the floor.  
- **watchFor:** No heading hard. Watch for slips; clean spills of water first.  
- **visualKey:** fun-balloon  
- **stub:** true

### fun-clean-up-race
- **title:** Clean-up race  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 3  
- **difficulty:** intro  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Turn putting equipment away into a timed team challenge.  
- **howTo:** Assign zones. On go, return items to homes. Stopwatch optional. Thank the group.  
- **watchFor:** No throwing equipment. Spot heavy mats — adults move those.  
- **visualKey:** fun-cleanup  
- **stub:** true

### fun-showcase-circle
- **title:** Showcase circle  
- **durationMinutesDefault:** 5  
- **difficulty:** easy  
- **tags:** `group`, `equipment-free`  
- **summary:** Optional share: one skill or shape each athlete is proud of today.  
- **howTo:** Circle. Volunteers show one thing. Clap for everyone. Passing is fine.  
- **watchFor:** Never force a performance. Keep the vibe supportive.  
- **visualKey:** fun-showcase  
- **stub:** true
